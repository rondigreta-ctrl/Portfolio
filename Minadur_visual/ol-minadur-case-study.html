Descend

A brand identity built from scratch for Ol Minadúr: a premium cheese aged in the historic mines of Dossena.

Role — Stakeholder coordination, Logo & packaging design
Methods — Producer interviews · Stakeholder workshops · Competitive analysis
Tools — Figma · Adobe Illustrator
Process

Many voices. One brief.

The project brought together a regional grant, a town, a cooperative, and a network of research partners: CNR (National Research Council), the University of Gastronomic Sciences, and IULM University on the technical and scientific side. I gathered input from producers, the cooperative, the mayor, and Dossena's tourism office, and translated it into a single creative brief.

The quality was already there.
The brand had to catch up to it.

The mark

One symbol. Four meanings.

01Crossed pickaxes

The universal sign of mining work and of the cellar where the cheese ages.

02Mountain line

Dossena's skyline, echoing the territorial brand of the valley.

03Golden dot

Representing “il paese nel sole” : Dossena's historical nickname.

04The custom Ú

A bespoke graphic accent, unreplicable with any standard font.

Packaging

From label to wheel.

The pelure

Designed to survive the cut.

The concentric system was designed around how the cheese is actually sold: each wedge carries a complete version of the brand: logo, name, symbol.
